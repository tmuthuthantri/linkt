# Linkt toll claims for Australian employees, built from your own statements

Drop in a Linkt statement or tax invoice. Each toll is read off the PDF, checked against your roster, leave and public holidays, and the work trips are marked on the original page.

Payments, card surcharges and totals stay out of the claim. GST is shown at one eleventh of each toll.

Upload statements

Open the sample statement

Statements are read in your browser and kept on this device only.

## Three steps from statement to claim

### Upload the statement
PDF statements and tax invoices, one or many at once.

### Check the marked tolls
Green rows go in the claim. Amber rows wait for your call.

### Export the claim package
A marked-up statement, a claim report with a signature block, Excel and CSV.
